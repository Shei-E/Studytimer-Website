Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to StudyTimer ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to providing a clean, aesthetic, and distraction-free Pomodoro study timer while respecting and protecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you visit or use our web application at studytimer.app.

2. Zero Server Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data on remote servers. StudyTimer is architected to operate entirely client-side on your local device.

3. Browser Local Storage (localStorage)

To deliver a continuous and personalized experience across visits without requiring an account or login, StudyTimer utilizes your browser's native localStorage mechanism. The following items are stored exclusively on your device:

  • Timer Durations & Preferences: Your customized work/break intervals and overtime mode settings.
  • Task List: Tasks you create, edit, or mark as completed.
  • Session History & Daily Log: Timestamps and study durations of completed sessions so you can track your daily focus time in the Summary popover.

This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

4. Cookies & Analytics

StudyTimer does not use tracking cookies or behavioral advertising trackers. Standard web server logs may temporarily record basic technical access data (such as IP address and browser type) for server security and uptime monitoring.

We use Google Fonts to render typography. Google Fonts are served according to Google's standard privacy practices.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times. You can wipe your session history and daily logs at any moment by clicking "Clear Sessions" or "Reset Stats" inside the StudyTimer Summary panel, or by clearing your browser's local storage and cache.

6. Children's Privacy

StudyTimer does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, our service is safe for users of all ages.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
